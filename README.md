# Chalchitra · Free mobile video editor

A free video editor that runs in your phone's browser. You can install it as an app, and all editing happens on the phone; nothing is uploaded.

**Features:** cut, trim, split, freeze frame, slow-mo and speed, crop, rotate and flip, clip animations, transitions, filters and effects, animated backgrounds, 3D effects and text, meme or scene from any description (built-in understanding of 60+ animals and things, or an optional AI helper with a free Gemini key), frog/dog/emoji characters, animal sounds, original cartoon characters, cartoon look, built-in music and sound-effects maker, real WebGL 3D (cube, card, sphere, tunnel, particles), meme templates, animated text and stickers, music and phone recordings, voiceover, voice effects, voice/music mix modes, vocals-only and no-vocals song split, MP3 download, animated overlays, sound-reactive animations, auto-saved projects, and export (save to gallery or share).

Best in Chrome on Android. Open the GitHub Pages link and choose **Add to Home screen**.
